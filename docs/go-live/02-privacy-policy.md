# EyeGo Privacy Policy

**Version:** 2026-09-01
**Last updated:** 1 September 2026

EyeGo ("we", "us") operates the EyeGo and EyeGo Driver mobile applications and
the EyeGo platform in Ghana. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we
share it with, and what you can ask us to do about it.

We are a data controller under Ghana's **Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)**
and are registered with the Data Protection Commission.

**Contact:** privacy@eyego.app

---

## 1. What we collect

### If you use EyeGo as a passenger

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your name and phone number | To create your account, and so your driver can identify you |
| Your email address, if you give us one | Receipts and account recovery. Optional |
| Your location while the app is open | To set your pickup point, show nearby vehicles, and follow your trip |
| Pickup and destination addresses, and your trip history | To provide the ride, show your past trips, and handle disputes |
| Payment details | Processed by Paystack. **We never see or store your full card number** |
| An emergency contact, if you add one | Only so we can alert them if you use the SOS feature. Optional |
| Your profile photo, if you add one | Optional |
| Messages you send your driver during a trip | To let you communicate, and to investigate complaints |
| Crash and diagnostic data | To fix the app |

### If you drive with EyeGo

Everything above, and:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your Ghana Card and driving licence | To verify you are permitted to drive commercially. Required by law and by us |
| Insurance and roadworthiness certificates, and their expiry dates | To check your cover is current. **If a certificate expires you cannot go online** |
| Vehicle details and photos | So passengers can identify your vehicle |
| Your location while you are online, and during a trip | To match you to nearby requests and to let your passengers see you approaching |
| Your payout account (mobile money or bank) | To pay you |
| Earnings, commission and wallet history | To pay you correctly, and for tax and accounting |

### What we do not collect

- We do **not** track you across other apps or websites.
- We do **not** sell your personal data. To anyone. Ever.
- We have **no advertising SDK and no third-party analytics SDK** in either app.
- The passenger app does **not** collect your location when it is closed.
- The driver app does **not** use background location. While you are on a trip
  it uses a foreground service with a notification you can see, so you always
  know when your location is being shared. It stops when the trip ends.

## 2. Why we are allowed to use it

| Basis | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Performing our contract with you** | Matching rides, navigation, payment, receipts, support |
| **Legal obligation** | Driver verification, tax and accounting records |
| **Legitimate interests** | Fraud prevention, safety, keeping the service working |
| **Your consent** | Contacts access, your emergency contact, optional notifications. You can withdraw it at any time |

---

## 3. Who we share it with

Only these, and only what each needs:

| Who | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your driver or passenger | First name, photo, rating, pickup and destination, and your location during the trip | So the ride can happen |
| **Paystack** | Payment identifiers, amount, and your mobile-money number where you pay that way | Processing payments |
| **Africa's Talking** | Your phone number and the message text | One-time codes, receipts, safety alerts |
| **Google (FCM) / Apple (APNs)** | A push token | Sending notifications |
| **Cloudinary** | Photos you upload | Storing them |
| **Mapbox / OpenStreetMap** | Coordinates | Maps, addresses and routes |
| **Sentry** | Crash reports and device model | Diagnosing crashes |
| Law enforcement or a regulator | What we are legally required to give | Only on a lawful request |

We do not share your phone number with the other party to a trip. In-app calls
go through a relay number.

---

## 4. How long we keep it

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account details | While your account is open |
| Trip and payment records | **6 years after the trip**, for tax, accounting and dispute purposes |
| Location traces attached to a trip | 12 months, then removed from the trip record |
| Driver verification documents | While you drive with us, and 6 years afterwards |
| Support conversations | 2 years |
| Crash reports | 90 days |

> **Deleting your account does not delete everything immediately.** Records we
> are required to keep for tax, accounting or an unresolved dispute are retained
> for the periods above, but are **anonymised**: they stop being connected to
> your name, your phone number or your account.

## 5. Your rights

Under Act 843 you may ask us to:

- **See** what we hold about you
- **Correct** anything wrong
- **Delete** your account and your data, subject to §4
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Withdraw consent** you previously gave
- **Complain** to the Data Protection Commission

**To delete your account:**
- Passenger app: Profile → Privacy → Delete account
- Driver app: Profile → Account deletion
- Or, without installing anything: **https://eyego.app/delete-account**

We respond within 30 days.

---

## 6. Security

Everything is encrypted in transit (TLS). Access to production data is limited
to named administrators who each sign in with their own account and two-factor
authentication — there are no shared logins — and every administrative action is
recorded in an audit log. Backups are encrypted before they leave our servers.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the
Data Protection Commission as the Act requires.

---

## 7. Children

EyeGo is not for under-18s. We do not knowingly collect their data. If you
believe a child has an account, contact privacy@eyego.app and we will remove it.

## 8. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, the apps will ask you to read
and accept the new version before you continue. We keep a record of which
version you accepted and when.
