## Two things that get listings rejected

**Screenshots that promise what the app does not do.** Every screenshot must be
a real screen. No mocked-up city, no invented feature, no stock photo of a car
with a UI pasted over it.

**A support email nobody reads.** Both stores check it, and a bounce is a
rejection.
