# EyeGo Terms of Service

**Version:** 2026-09-01
**Effective:** 1 September 2026

---

## 1. Who we are, and what EyeGo is

EyeGo is a technology platform that connects passengers who want a ride with
independent drivers who provide one, in Ghana.

**We do not provide transport.** We connect you with a driver who does. The
driver decides whether to accept your request; we decide who to offer it to.

Using the apps means you accept these terms. If you do not, do not use them.

---

## 2. Your account

You must be 18 or older, give accurate information, and keep your sign-in
secure. One account per person. You are responsible for what happens on your
account.

We may suspend an account for fraud, for abuse or threats towards a driver or
passenger, for repeated cancellations or no-shows, or where the law requires it.

---

## 3. Booking, and what you pay

Before you confirm, the app shows you a fare made up of a base fare, a distance
and time rate for the tier you chose, plus any applicable fees. That price is
what you are charged, unless the trip changes — a new destination, a detour you
ask for, or waiting time beyond the free allowance.

You pay by cash, mobile money, card or your EyeGo wallet. Card and mobile money
payments are processed by **Paystack**; we do not store your card number.

You receive a receipt in the app, and by SMS when a trip completes.

---

## 4. Cancelling

You may cancel free of charge before a driver accepts, or within the free window
shown in the app after acceptance.

A cancellation fee may apply if you cancel after that window, or if you are not
at the pickup point when the driver has waited the period the app shows. The fee
is shown before it is charged.

A driver may cancel; where they do, you are not charged, and we look for another
driver.

---

## 5. Shared and group rides

On a shared trip you book a seat rather than the vehicle. A seat you have
selected is held for a limited period shown in the app; if it is not paid for
within that period the seat returns to sale.

Where a trip has a minimum occupancy, the driver may wait until it is met or,
having acknowledged it, depart under it.

---

## 6. Safety

**Verify My Ride.** You can require a PIN which you show your driver before the
trip begins. Your driver never sees this code — only you do — which is what
makes it proof.

**SOS.** Both apps have an emergency button that calls the emergency services
and, if you choose, alerts EyeGo and your emergency contact with your location.
**In a genuine emergency, call the emergency services first.** We are not an
emergency service and cannot substitute for one.

**Share your trip.** You can send someone a link that shows your journey in real
time.

You agree not to bring anything illegal into a vehicle, to behave lawfully and
respectfully towards your driver, and to wear a seatbelt.

---

## 7. Ratings

Passengers and drivers rate each other after a trip. Ratings are shown as an
average, never as individual comments attributed to a person. Persistently low
ratings may end your access to the platform.

---

## 8. Your EyeGo wallet

The wallet holds a balance you top up in advance and spend on rides. **It is not
a bank account. It earns no interest, and it is not a deposit.** Passenger wallet
balances are for paying for rides on the platform.

Driver wallet balances hold earnings and can be withdrawn to a mobile money or
bank account, subject to the minimum shown in the app.

---

## 9. If you drive with EyeGo

**You are an independent contractor, not an employee.** You decide when to work,
whether to accept a request, and what route to take, subject to the law and to
reasonable passenger requests. Nothing in these terms creates employment, agency
or partnership.

You must:

- hold a valid driving licence of the correct class for carrying passengers
- hold valid insurance covering passengers, and a valid roadworthiness
  certificate — **the app will not let you go online if either has expired**
- keep your vehicle roadworthy, clean and correctly registered
- upload accurate documents and update them before they expire
- comply with all applicable law

**Commission.** We charge commission on each trip, at the rate published in the
driver app. It is deducted from your earnings or from your driver wallet. Where
a passenger pays cash, you collect the fare and the commission is taken from
your wallet — which is why a minimum wallet balance is required to go online.

We may remove you from the platform for expired documents, dangerous driving,
fraud, mistreating a passenger, or persistently poor ratings.

To the fullest extent Ghanaian law allows, we are not liable for the conduct of

for anything lost in a vehicle — though we will try to help you recover it.

Nothing here limits liability for death or personal injury caused by our
negligence, for fraud, or for anything that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Where we are liable, our liability is limited to the value of the trip in
question.
